Error guide & rubric ↗

Before you begin

You will post-edit a DeepL Spanish translation of a USU Extension fact sheet against the English original. For each segment, identify the errors, classify them, and propose your edit. You will then evaluate the ChatGPT review, report missing source materials, and log your time. Your work is sent to the project spreadsheet when you submit.

This tool records your error analysis. Your final edited file is still the Word document with Track Changes turned on, as agreed with the Extension team. You will paste its link in Step 6.

Focus areas

AccuracyMeaning, omissions, additions, numbers, units, calculations.
TerminologyCorrect agricultural and pesticide terms for the field.
ReadabilityClear, grammatical, plain-language Spanish.
ConsistencyThe same term and form of address (usted) throughout.
Cultural & linguistic appropriatenessFit for Spanish-speaking audiences in Utah and the U.S.
FormattingLayout, figures, captions, lists, headings, references.

Add a new fact sheet

Type the fact sheet name, then paste the English original and the DeepL Spanish. The tool splits both into segments. Check that each English line sits next to its Spanish line before you start.

Check the alignment. Each line below is one segment. Edit the lines so English line 5 matches Spanish line 5, and so on: press Enter to split a segment, or delete a line break to join two. Rows highlighted in orange have a missing partner.

Severity guide

SeverityUse when…Points
CriticalThe error could lead to unsafe handling, exposure, a wrong dose or concentration, or noncompliance with the label.25
MajorMeaning changes or becomes unclear, and a reader is likely to misunderstand or need to reread.5
MinorMeaning is intact, but there is a grammar, convention, consistency or style problem a professional would fix.1
PreferentialThe MT is acceptable and your change is a preference. Record it, but be careful not to over-edit.0
Source issueThe problem is in the English original, such as an arithmetic error or ambiguity. Query the author. This does not count against the MT.0

The project team generated an AI review of this translation. Treat it as a second opinion, not as an answer key. AI reviews can be right, partly right, or wrong. For each item, decide whether you accept, modify, or reject the suggestion, and explain why. If you modify it, write your own version.
